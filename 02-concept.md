# MOVA: Moving Our Young Ahead

- **One-liner:** MOVA turns students who miss class into mentors that younger students count on, because when someone needs you, you show up.
- **Tagline:** Show up. Show the way.

## The concept
**Students who are missing class become mentors.** Giving them responsibility for younger students gives them a reason to show up, and the app turns that responsibility into incentives.

## How it works
1. Students who are chronically absent or slipping (10th–12th grade) are invited to become **mentors**.
2. Each mentor is paired with **up to 5 mentees**, ideally younger students (for example, incoming 9th graders) who look up to them.
3. To be a good mentor, you have to **be there**: mentors check in on their mentees, meet them, and help them through the day. Their own attendance becomes part of the role.
4. The app tracks it and **rewards it**: points, forgiving streaks, badges, crew leaderboards ranked by improvement, and progress toward **service or leadership credit**, verified by a teacher advisor.

## Why it works (evidence)
- **IDRA Valued Youth Partnership** has run this model since 1984: it identifies students at risk of dropping out and makes them tutors for younger, struggling students.
  - It has kept 98% of its tutors in school, more than 33,000 students.
  - Tutors show higher attendance, lower discipline rates, stronger academic skills, and higher self-esteem.
  - In a 1989 evaluation, 1 of 101 tutors (1%) dropped out, compared with 11 of 93 comparison students (12%).
- **What's new with MOVA:** Valued Youth is an in-person class. MOVA brings the model into a phone app, with daily check-ins, catch-up challenges, AI support for mentors, gamified incentives and a simple advisor view, so it can scale across Michigan schools.

## Scope
- Michigan high schools, piloting in Detroit.
- Mentors: 10th–12th grade. Mentees: mainly 9th grade.
- No college-based incentives; rewards are school, community and career based.
