# Features

## Mentee (Kayla)
- **One-tap morning check-in:** All good · Bus is late · Family stuff · Not feeling it. Private by default; help is offered only if she wants it.
- **Forgiving streaks:** a missed day pauses a streak instead of resetting it.
- **Catch-up challenges:** short, visual, Kahoot-style questions at her level (AI-generated), with no penalty for wrong answers.
- **Her mentor:** messages, cheers, and a "Talk to Jaylen" option when things are hard.

## Mentor (Jaylen)
- **Crew code (onboarding):** 8 promises: show up, be kind, keep it private, meet at school, talk in MOVA, tell an adult, log honestly, be a role model. Parent/guardian also signs at enrollment.
- **Crew dashboard:** 5 mentee bubbles colored by status, "who needs you", crew leaderboard ranked by improvement.
- **MOVA tip (AI nudge):** "Kayla said 'family stuff' today. Check in kindly. Don't ask why." Mentors never see the reason a student is out.
- **Drafted for you (AI):** a kind, editable message, plus a safety check before it's sent.
- **Daily log (about 1 minute):** who, when (start/end; minutes calculated), support given, challenge mentioned (private), details.
- **Mentor support:** "I need backup", "I'm worried about a mentee" (goes to an adult), tips for tough talks, cover-my-crew, weekly mentor circle.

## Rewards (7 incentives)
| Reward | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| Crew pizza party | Your whole crew shows up 5 days straight |
| Skip-the-line lunch pass | On time 10 days |
| MOVA hoodie drop | Earn 5 badges |
| Game day tickets | 2,000 points |
| Principal's letter for your résumé | Mentor for 8 weeks |
| Leadership credit | 40 mentoring hours, advisor-approved |
| Summer job fast-track | Finish the semester as a mentor |

Rewards that are 80%+ done move to an "Almost there 🔥" section with a pulsing glow and "1 more day!" copy. Unlocking one triggers a full-screen celebration that credits the mentor and the whole crew.

## Advisor (Mr. Robinson)
- **FERPA acknowledgement** before any student data (renews every school year).
- **Today at a glance:** Everyone / Mentees / Mentors views; help requests with Reply; challenges this week (private); mentor logs to review; missing-log nudge; mentor activity; AI weekly summary from totals only.
- **Log review:** approve minutes toward leadership credit, ask the mentor a question, or loop in the counselor.
- **Mentor profile:** attendance change, log streak, credit progress, mentees, recent logs.
