# Research brief: Why Detroit high schoolers miss school

*Compiled Nov 13, 2026 for the Michigan DevFest Hackathon (education focus).*

## Scale of the problem
- DPSCD chronic absenteeism: 60.9% in 2024-25, about 60% in 2025-26. That is double the state average. (Chalkbeat)
- DPSCD high schools: 64% chronically absent in 2023-24, 54% by April 2026 after the cash incentive. (Chalkbeat)
- Some neighborhood high schools are far worse. In 2023-24, Henry Ford was at 88% and East English Village at 94%. (WXYZ)
- Statewide, 12th graders have the highest rate of any grade, about 35.7%. (Bridge Michigan)
- What's at stake: more than a quarter of Detroiters aged 16-24 are neither in school nor working, the 2nd-highest rate nationally. (Chalkbeat/Youth Today)

## Why they miss
1. **Transportation.** DPSCD has no yellow buses for most high schoolers, so they take DDOT city buses. Students report buses are late, don't stop near home, or require two buses.
   - An Outlier Media analysis found more than 40% of DPSCD high schools are served by buses that run infrequently or don't match bell times.
   - City buses have been free for K-12 students since April 2026 (the "Ride to Rise" pilot), but long waits remain.
2. **Mental health.** 43% of Michigan school leaders rank it a top barrier (Detroit PEER). Teens who report struggling miss about 12 more days a year (USC).
3. **Family responsibilities and money pressure.** Teens care for siblings or older relatives, work to support their families, and deal with housing instability (MSU research on Detroit and Flint teens).
4. **Disengagement.** Teens who care "a lot" about school miss about 10 fewer days (USC).
5. **Key insight (USC).** Less common reasons (caregiving, transportation, mental health, suspensions) account for the most missed days among the students they affect.

## What's been tried
- **$100/week perfect-attendance gift cards.** About 12,800 of 15,247 high schoolers received at least one, and the high school rate fell 10 points, but it is "still not at scale" for students missing 45+ days.
- **Free DDOT rides and a yellow-bus pilot.**

## The gap
- Schools know *how much* students miss, not *why* (Jeremy Singer, Wayne State).
- Researchers recommend tracking absences by root cause and targeting precise supports.
